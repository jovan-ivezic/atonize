Who we are

AT Medics was established in 2004 and is a leading GP provider of quality primary healthcare services in London. We have 26 practices spread across 13 CCG’s, and care for 140000 patients. 3 of our practices have been awarded an “outstanding” rating by the CQC, and eight practices have achieved the RCGP Quality Practice Award.
