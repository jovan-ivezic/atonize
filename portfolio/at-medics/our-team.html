Susan Sinclair:

Chief Executive Officer

Susan is responsible for leading the strategic and operational functions of AT Medics, reporting directly to and advising the Board of Directors. As CEO, Susan also leads the organisations Senior Management Team, comprising of operational and corporate managers, driving quality and change programmes through the organisation.

Susan was previously Director of Strategy at West Middlesex University Hospital where she led ondevelopment, transition, assurance and business case implementation for the Trust. She has also been a Senior Operational Leader in the acute sector. Focussed on integration through her role as a Deputy Director in the North West London-wide Strategy and Transformation team, Susan has also led the NWL Integrated Care Pilot directing cross provider care delivery models. Susan is ultimately responsible for the day to day operations across the organisation.

Dr Tarek Radwan

Director Lead Clinical Transformation

Dr Radwan graduated from St George’s, University of London in 1999 with Distinction, an honour given to the top 5 in each year. He then spent 3 years training in Paediatrics and Neonatal medicine and gained the Membership of the Royal College of Paediatrics and Child Health in 2001. He attained his Membership of the Royal College of General Practitioners in 2005 with Distinction. Dr Radwan is an experienced GP specialist trainer for the London Specialty School of General Practice. He has also been a GP Director of SW Lambeth healthcare, he has been instrumental in transforming clinical care in Streatham, Hackney and Whitechapel and was awarded GP trainer of the year 2009 by Kings College Hospital.

His specific interests are paediatrics, cardiovascular disease and diabetes.

Dr Hasnain Abbasi

Director Lead Finance and Human Resources

Dr Abbasi graduated from St. George’s, University of London gaining a Merit Award for “Outstanding Performance” in Medical MB finals. He attained Membership to the Royal College of General Practitioners with Merit Award in 2005. He is Joint Clinical Lead Director in East and South East London and is the Finance Lead for AT Medics as well as being involved with GP Leadership Development.

Dr Abbasi is an experienced GP specialist trainer for the London Specialty School of General Practice and he also supervises and assesses undergraduate students at St. George’s, University of London. He was elected by Lambeth GPs to the CCG governing body and chairs the Primary Care Development Programme. Dr Abbasi has successfully led the pan-Lambeth Federation bid for the Prime Minister’s Challenge Fund, securing £3.5mil for Lambeth residents.

He is now Joint Chair of the steering group leading extended access implementation in Lambeth.
